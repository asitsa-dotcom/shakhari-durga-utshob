

# শাঁখারিটোলা দুর্গোৎসব সমিতি — Website Plan

## Overview
A clean, modern, fully responsive Bengali community website for the Shankaritola Durgotsab Samiti — a registered cultural organization in Kolkata. The site will use a festive theme with Deep Red, Gold, and White colors, Bengali-first typography, and a mobile-first design approach.

---

## Pages & Features

### 1. Home Page (হোম)
- **Hero Section** with a large traditional Durga image background, overlay with the samiti name, subtitle about culture & social bonding, and registration details
- **Theme Song 2025** section with embedded YouTube video
- **Theme Song 2024** section with embedded YouTube video

### 2. About Us (আমাদের কথা)
- Professional Bengali description of the organization's mission and history
- **আমাদের লক্ষ্য** subsection with 4 goals displayed as icon cards
- Organization address displayed prominently

### 3. Activities (আমাদের কার্যক্রম)
- 8 activity cards (Durga Puja, Khuti Puja, Dol Utsav, Independence Day, Republic Day, Rabindra Jayanti, Sports, Social Programs) each with a short 2-3 line Bengali description
- Image placeholders for future gallery additions

### 4. Media Gallery (গ্যালারি)
- Photo grid layout with placeholder images
- Video section supporting YouTube embeds
- Clean, fast-loading layout

### 5. Leadership (সমিতির নেতৃত্ব)
- Card layout for committee positions: President, Vice-President, Secretary, Joint Secretary, Treasurer, and Executive Members
- Placeholder names and avatar images, no contact details

### 6. Live Page (লাইভ সম্প্রচার)
- Heading and descriptive text about live broadcast during Durga Puja
- YouTube Live embed placeholder section

### 7. Contact Page (যোগাযোগ)
- Simple form with Name, Mobile Number, and Message fields
- Google Map placeholder for location
- Organization address

### Navigation & Layout
- Responsive top navigation bar with Bengali page names and the samiti logo/name
- Mobile hamburger menu
- Sticky footer with copyright, registration number, and designer credit (Asit Kumar Sarkar)

### Design System
- **Colors**: Deep Red (#8B0000) as primary, Gold (#FFD700) as accent, White background
- **Typography**: Bengali-first fonts (Noto Sans Bengali / Hind Siliguri from Google Fonts)
- **Style**: Minimal animations, festive but elegant, fast-loading
- **No backend needed** — purely frontend, no payment system, no auth
